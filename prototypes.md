# Labelling Game with Mannequin
<div class="video-grid">
<iframe width="560" height="315" 
  src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/dCXQCzwJbTg" 
  title="YouTube video player" 
  frameborder="0" 
  allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" 
  allowfullscreen>
</iframe>
<p>This prototype is the first iteration of a labelling game in immersive augmented reality. It focuses on the skeleton of human beings, to learn about how the individual bones are called. Two players wearing a Microsoft HoloLens can together label a mannequin, which is overlayed with virtual bones. Choosing from a virtual list of names for the bones, the players can work in tandem to find the correct combination of bones and names. The players can directly interact, handing each other labels, or taking them from each other. This video was filmed at the Hochschule RheinMain in Wiesbaden, Germany. Credits to Lorena Müller and Leonie Ferdinand for participating and agreeing to being filmed. <br>
Year: 2024</p>
</div>
